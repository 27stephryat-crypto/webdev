Competition

Overview

There are many students that have similar skills, qualifications, and experiences that I have. Many students use similar portfolio websites to portray their skills and strengths. These may include other high school students looking to stand out to their future employers and colleges. When building this portfolio, I looked at other student portfolio and resume websites to better understand what they do well and how they can improve. This helped me get ideas for how to make my own site more professional and effective.

What They Did Right

Many students' portfolio websites are well organized and easy to navigate. They include sections highlighting their skills, experience, and achievements, which make it simple for employers to navigate and look up easily. Some of the portfolio websites were clean, simple, with minimal color schemes, and good formatting. This made the content on the page easy to see and navigate. Some included graphics and photos. Many had personal photos and this made their portfolios stand out and personable.

What they did wrong

Many of the student’s portfolio websites were cluttered with information. There were too many pictures and graphics. This made it very distracting for the viewer. Some had too much content information and complex fonts, which makes it hard for those to view in a hurry, while others had general layouts, which made them dull. Some had all dark color schemes, which I found hard to look at after a while. I think a lighter color scheme is more appealing. Many did not include personal achievements, honors or awards, as well as personal information highlighting extracurricular activities and hobbies.

Why my Website is More Effective

My portfolio website is better because it balances professionalism with a personal touch. It clearly shows who I am, what I can do, and the strengths I bring. My awards, certifications, leadership, personal achievements, and current lifeguard experience is highlighted clearly. Instead of being overwhelming and confusing, my design is clean, simple, straightforward, and easy for future employers or colleges to navigate.
