Audience

Overview

This portfolio website is designed mainly for those interested in hiring me, recruiting for colleges, or learning more about my background. The primary audience includes employers, managers, and college admissions staff who want to review my skills, achievements, and experiences. It may also be viewed by teachers and community members that are interested in my education and leadership involvement.

Geolocation

Most people who will use this website live locally in Southern Utah or nearby communities. These are the areas where jobs, community involvement, and volunteer opportunities are most common for me. Colleges in Utah may also view this site.

Age Range

The primary age group for this audience will be around 25-55 years old. Employers, managers, teachers, community leaders, and college admissions staff are commonly in this age range.

Gender

This portfolio site is intended for all genders who are interested in me for job opportunities, community involvement, or college admissions.

Interests and Needs

The people who are viewing this website are looking for someone who is hardworking, dependable, respectful, and eager to learn. They may want to see evidence of leadership, work experience, academic success, and community involvement before hiring someone. My portfolio website is designed for the viewer to find this information easily.

Why This Audience Matters

Understanding my audience and what they are looking for in a candidate, helps me design a professional and organized website. Since employers and college staff often review many applicants, it is important that my website is professional, clean, and easy to navigate.

Summary

Overall, this website is for anyone who would want to learn more about me and evaluate whether I would be a good candidate for job, college, or community opportunities. My goal is to leave a good impression and show that I am responsible and hardworking.
